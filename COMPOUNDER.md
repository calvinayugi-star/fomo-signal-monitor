## Rules in effect

- **Lists:** Graduated (pump.fun) and Trending (GeckoTerminal). Tokens that graduated within 72h get +0 score, older graduates 0, Trending-only tokens −0; market cap ≥ 50M −15.
- **Score (0–100 before adjustments):** 1h buys/sells 30, price rise in the hour (up to +10%) 15, 6h buys/sells 15, last 15 min volume vs the average 15 min 15, volume acceleration 15 (0 for pairs under ~1.5h), liquidity growth 10; minus 3 per 1% DEX round trip.
- **Price:** none for pairs under 1h old (since 2026-10-21; their 1h/6h change is the change since the pool opened), otherwise 1h change -10% to +25% and 6h -15% to +100%; no buy if up more than 25% in the last 15 min (since 2026-10-24), no 5-min limit. The score rewards a 1h rise up to +10%.
- **Demand building:** last hour's volume ≥ 1× the hours before, buys/sells ≥ 1.15 (1h) and ≥ 1 (6h) but ≤ 5 (bots), ≥ 30 buys and $5k volume in the hour, liquidity not shrinking.
- **Quality:** liquidity ≥ $10k and ≥ 50× the position, market cap ≥ 100k, pair age since the pool opened (time since graduation for pump.fun tokens): Conservative Compounder 60–4320 min, Aggressive Compounder 60–4320 min (since 2026-10-24; the volume-acceleration check is skipped for pairs under ~1.5h, which have no earlier hours), the monitor's safety checks, no top-10 share ≤ 8% on tokens under 72h (bundled wallets), round trip ≤ 4% at the real size, score ≥ 45.
- **Exit:** only at the run's target (+13.93% or +20% net, after fees and slippage) or at the run's goal, checked on 1-minute candles, so the sale happens at the first minute the price gets there even though the job runs every 15 min. No stop loss. Time limit (since 2026-10-27, 1h since 2026-10-28): a position that hasn't reached its target 1h after the fill is sold at the last price before then, and the run searches again.
- **Crash filters (since 2026-10-28):** no buy if liquidity under 5% of market cap, or, vs an earlier holder reading (10 min to 3h old), holders falling, the developer's share down 0.5+ points, or the top-10 share down 5+ points.
- **Outcome tracker (since 2026-10-28):** every token a run evaluates inside its age window, bought or rejected, is re-priced 15 and 60 min later: data/candidate-outcomes-YYYY-MM.jsonl, one file per month.
- Paper fill 3 min after the decision (cancelled if the token doesn't trade for 6h). No re-buy of a token within 24h of selling it. A warning is sent if a held token has no trades for 24h.

Every hourly decision, the reject counts and the candidates that passed the quick checks are logged in [data/compounder-hourly.jsonl](data/compounder-hourly.jsonl).
